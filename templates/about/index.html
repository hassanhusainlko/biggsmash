About Biggsmash Outreach

Strategy-driven, people-centric digital communication agency

Who We Are

Biggsmash Outreach (OPC) Pvt. Ltd. is a new-age outreach and communications agency with a proven track record of working with government bodies and public institutions. We specialize in strategy-driven, people-centric digital communication — combining content, design, and data to help public bodies reach citizens more effectively.

Our team of experienced professionals brings together expertise in political strategy, digital marketing, data analytics, and grassroots mobilization to deliver comprehensive solutions that make a real impact.

Our Mission

We are committed to bridging the gap between government institutions and citizens through innovative digital communication strategies. Our mission is to empower public bodies with the tools, strategies, and support they need to communicate effectively with the people they serve.

We believe in integrity, transparency, and results-driven approaches that build trust and foster meaningful engagement between institutions and communities.

How We Work

Our approach combines data-driven insights with creative excellence. We begin by understanding your unique challenges and objectives, then develop customized strategies that deliver measurable results. Our process includes:

1. Discovery & Research - Understanding your goals, audience, and challenges
2. Strategy Development - Creating tailored communication plans
3. Implementation - Executing campaigns with precision
4. Monitoring & Optimization - Continuous improvement based on data
5. Reporting - Transparent performance tracking and insights

About the Founder

Dhiraj Kumar

Founder & Director, Biggsmash Outreach (OPC) Pvt. Ltd.

“Effective communication is not merely about transmitting a message—it is about shaping perception, engineering trust, and driving measurable action at every tier of society.”

Dhiraj Kumar is a seasoned public communications advisor and strategist, and the visionary leader behind Biggsmash Outreach (OPC) Pvt. Ltd. With over 8 years of deep domain experience in campaign architecture, political strategy, and corporate advisory, Dhiraj has pioneered a modern approach to integrated communications in India.

Prior to founding Biggsmash Outreach in 2022, Dhiraj worked with leading consultancy firms, orchestrating high-stakes political campaigns, public policy rollouts, and institutional perception strategies. Recognizing that public figures, corporate entities, and government bodies were struggling with fragmented, multi-vendor communication models, he engineered Biggsmash Outreach to serve as a comprehensive, single-window strategic partner.

Under his leadership, Biggsmash Outreach has evolved into a multi-regional power center operating across Patna (HQ), Agartala (Northeast Regional Hub), and Gurugram (NCR Regional Hub). His sharp analytical capabilities, deep understanding of regional demographic nuances, and commitment to data-backed execution have established Biggsmash as a trusted partner for public leaders, business enterprises, and state institutions across India.
